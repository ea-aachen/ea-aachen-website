EAGxBerlin is a three-day effective altruism conference at Urania Berlin, with talks, workshops and a lot of 1:1 conversations with other people in EA.

A few of us from EA Aachen are going together, and we still have **two spots left** in our group. If you'd like to join, write to us at [info@ea-aachen.org](mailto:info@ea-aachen.org) or in our [WhatsApp group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/LYduMS20H9j7voTgijQGqW).

To attend, you need to apply yourself on the [EAGxBerlin page](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/ea-global/events/eagxberlin-2026) by **25 October 2026**. There's an option to attend for free, and you can request travel support in the application.
