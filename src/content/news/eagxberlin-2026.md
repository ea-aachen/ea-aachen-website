EAGxBerlin is a three-day effective altruism conference at Urania Berlin, with talks, workshops and a lot of 1:1 conversations with other people in EA. A few of us from EA Aachen are going together.

Going too, or want to join us? Write us an email at [info@ea-aachen.org](mailto:info@ea-aachen.org). All details and the application are on the [EAGxBerlin page](https://www.effectivealtruism.org/ea-global/events/eagxberlin-2026).
